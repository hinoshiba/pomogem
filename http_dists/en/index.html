WHY MASS?

Beyond a chart.
Something you can feel.

Focused time disappears from view the moment it ends. PomoGem turns that time into weight that feels almost tangible, instead of monitoring it. On one iPhone, the same total time always makes the same mass, whether your sessions are short or long. Tap the gems to make them bounce. Tilt your phone to watch them roll.

01

It stays with you.

Take a day off. Your gems stay right where they are. There are no streaks to count.

02

Every minute counts equally.

One minute always equals 10g. Progress is based on your total focused time, not the number of sessions.

03

No guilt.

Manually added sessions also remain as a record of your effort, clearly distinguished from timed sessions.

AFTER IT FILLS

A full jar
is just the beginning.

The jar fills with light every 2.50kg (250 minutes), as many times as you keep going, and counts each fill. Any extra carries over to the next cycle. Moving gems are combined into groups of 10 to make room. Your total mass and every individual record stay intact.

10 individual gems→One ×10 grouped gem→Build up to ×100

Home shows your current jar. The weekly view puts measured mass first, and a long break is suggested every 1,000g (100 minutes). Open “Accumulation” in the menu for an overview of grouped gems and monthly jars. In “Accumulation Plan,” estimate future jars by changing the period, sessions per week, and 10, 25, or 60 minute sessions. Estimates are not saved to your records or iCloud and reset when you close the view.

PRIVATE, AND YOURS

Choose where
your data lives.

At first launch, “Sync with iCloud” and “This iPhone Only” are presented as equal choices. With “This iPhone Only,” core features work without an Apple Account or a network connection, and your data is not sent automatically. If you choose and confirm iCloud, theme names, achievement notes, records, settings, and running timers sync through your Apple Account’s private iCloud storage. In Version 1.0, you cannot change storage later, and deleting the app removes records stored only on this iPhone. JSON exports cannot be reimported or used for migration. There is no account registration, analytics SDK, advertising tracking, or transmission to our own server.

BEFORE YOUR FIRST GEM

Before your
first gem.

Start with one gem and go at your own pace. Here are answers to common questions.

What can I use for free?

25, 45, 60, and 90 minute timers, gem records, themes, history, sharing, and optional iCloud sync are free. Pro is a one-time purchase that adds custom durations from 1 to 360 minutes and month stamps on grouped gems. Compare Free & Pro features

Does the timer work with my screen locked?

Yes. The timer continues while your screen is locked. If you allow notifications, you will receive one when the session ends. Notifications do not show the theme name. You can change notification settings later.

Is it okay if I skip a day?

Your gems do not disappear when you take a day off. PomoGem keeps your focused time without counting streaks. You can also add time spent without a timer as a manual record, distinguished from timed sessions.

Do I need an account or internet access?

You do not need to register a PomoGem account. Choose “This iPhone Only” at first launch to use core features without an Apple Account or a network connection. “Sync with iCloud” requires the same Apple Account and a network connection.

Can I keep my records when I change phones?

If you chose “Sync with iCloud” at first launch, you can continue your records on a supported iPhone using the same Apple Account. In Version 1.0, you cannot change storage later. Deleting the app removes records stored with “This iPhone Only,” and exported JSON cannot be reimported or used for migration. Learn more about storage
