PomoGem Pro is a one-time, non-consumable In-App Purchase. It unlocks custom focus durations from 1 minute 00 seconds to 360 minutes 00 seconds beyond the free 25-, 45-, 60-, and 90-minute presets, month labels on grouped gems, and unlimited learning apps for optional Screen Time recording (five apps on the free plan). Black distraction-gem app selection is unlimited without purchase.

Share cards retain the PomoGem logo and https://pomogem.hinoshiba.com/ for Free and Pro users. There is no subscription, free trial, automatic renewal, external payment, account login, or custom purchase server. This update uses the existing product com.hinoshiba.pomogem.pro.lifetime; previous purchasers receive the added benefit through their entitlement.

1. On first launch, choose “このiPhoneだけに保存” and confirm “このiPhoneだけで始める” to use basic features without an Apple Account or network. The optional iCloud choice also reaches the same purchase screen.

3. Open Home's top-right menu, then Settings and the “ポモジェムPro” row. The same paywall opens when choosing a custom duration while Free.

5. “購入を復元” is at the bottom of the same screen.
6. On a supported iPhone, Settings → “スクリーンタイム” requests individual Family Controls access. After authorizing it, “勉強のgem” allows five learning apps for Free, and more than five after purchase or restore. Select a theme, enable recording, and save. Categories and websites are not accepted. The separate “黒いgem” selection is unlimited for everyone.
7. Learning-app use is combined in confirmed 10-minute steps (100 grams), with OS delivery delays possible. Black gems stay outside study totals and sharing. Screen Time authorization is optional and is separate from the purchase; denying it does not prevent use of the timers or other Pro features.
