PomoGem Pro is a one-time, non-consumable In-App Purchase. It unlocks every focus duration from 1 to 360 minutes beyond the free 25-, 45-, 60-, and 90-minute presets, plus month labels on grouped pebbles. Share cards retain the PomoGem logo and https://pomogem.hinoshiba.com/ for Free and Pro users. There is no subscription, free trial, automatic renewal, external payment, account login, or custom purchase server.

Review steps:

1. On first launch, choose “iCloudに保存して同期” and confirm with “確認して続ける”. Choosing “このiPhoneだけに保存” also reaches the same app and purchase screen.
2. On the optional trial-pebble page, tap “次へ” to skip it, then select one initial theme from the combined study/work list.
3. On Home, open the top-right menu, then Settings.
4. Tap the entire “ポモジェムPro” row. The same paywall opens when choosing a custom duration while Free.
5. The paywall displays the storefront price returned by StoreKit. “価格・提供条件・販売者情報を確認” opens the pre-purchase terms and seller disclosure before the purchase button.
6. “購入を復元” is at the bottom of the same screen.

Use the paywall showing the live Product.displayPrice as the IAP review screenshot. This first non-consumable is submitted with app version 1.0. No review account is required.
