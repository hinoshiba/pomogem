Turn focused time into colorful gems.

Study and work time becomes visible progress. Earned gems stay when you take a day off. The app is in English and Japanese. Support is currently provided in Japanese.

CHOOSE A THEME AND START

Choose a theme and a free 25-, 45-, 60-, or 90-minute timer. Finish a session and a gem drops into your jar with sound and haptic feedback. Each completed minute becomes 10 grams: 25 minutes adds 250 grams.

OPTIONAL SCREEN TIME GEMS

Choose learning apps and a theme. Each confirmed 10 minutes of combined use adds a 100-gram gem. Select up to five learning apps for free, or unlimited apps with Pro. This uses OS threshold notifications, so delivery can be delayed. Partial periods under 10 minutes are not added, and learning-app monitoring pauses while a focus timer runs.

You can also choose apps you want to use less. Each combined 10 minutes adds a black obstacle gem. This selection is unlimited for everyone. Black gems combine only with black gems; they do not count toward study time, study mass, rewards, or sharing, and never subtract earned study gems. The same app cannot belong to both selections. You choose whether to authorize and enable recording.

POMOGEM PRO

A one-time, non-consumable In-App Purchase unlocks custom timers from 1 minute 00 seconds to 360 minutes 00 seconds, month stamps on crystals, and unlimited learning apps for Screen Time recording. Enter minutes and seconds or use scroll wheels. Update both devices before handing off timers set with seconds. No subscription, trial, or automatic renewal. The purchase screen shows your App Store price.

YOUR STORAGE CHOICE

At first launch, choose private iCloud sync or this iPhone only. Device-only mode works without an Apple Account or network and never uploads automatically. If you confirm iCloud, theme names, achievement notes, records, settings, and the active timer use your Apple Account's private CloudKit storage. Initial loading and resuming sync require online checks.

An eligible, previously verified local copy can be used offline in iCloud mode; changes wait on this iPhone for sync. Verified account changes or conflicting history can block access to protect data. Reopening the app may be required. A connection check does not prove all records have uploaded.

Settings lets you enable iCloud using its data to replace device-only records, or disable it by copying verified data to this iPhone while retaining iCloud data. In iCloud mode, you may also preview the cloud contents and explicitly confirm replacing this device's data, including unsent changes. Histories are not merged. Replacing all iCloud data with device data remains unavailable.

Transfers require a connection and restarts as instructed. Do not uninstall during a transfer. Uninstalling loses device-only records and unsent changes. Exported JSON cannot be reimported for restoration or migration.

Screen Time app selections, pending thresholds, and black gems stay on this iPhone and are excluded from JSON export and storage transfers. Imported learning records follow your chosen storage, including private iCloud sync, but are excluded from shared images, GIFs, captions, and JSON export. Unverified summaries are excluded from sharing and export. Diagnostics stay on-device without app tokens or gem counts. No advertising, tracking, analytics SDK, or developer server.
