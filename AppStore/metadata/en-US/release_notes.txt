- During a focus, going to the Home Screen or another app now pauses the timer from the moment you left; tap Resume when you come back to continue. If notifications are allowed, up to five reminders say the focus is paused. On an iPhone with a passcode, locking usually keeps the timer running; without a passcode, locking also pauses it. Turn the pause or its reminders off in Settings.
- Added optional Screen Time recording: each confirmed 10 minutes of combined learning-app use adds a 100-gram gem. Choose up to five learning apps for free, or unlimited apps with Pro. OS notification delivery may be delayed.
- Selected distraction apps can add black gems. App selection is unlimited for everyone. Black gems combine only with black gems and stay outside study totals and sharing.
- Screen Time settings remain responsive while saving, with safeguards against duplicate saves and duplicate records during changes or resets.
- In iCloud mode, preview cloud contents and explicitly confirm replacing this device's data, including unsent changes, from Settings. Replacing all iCloud data with device data remains unavailable.
- Improved launch retries and offline-access checks. Missing iCloud management information is no longer incorrectly described as another device replacing the data.
