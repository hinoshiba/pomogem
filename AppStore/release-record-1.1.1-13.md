## User-visible changes and compatibility

- Screen Time learning apps can each use a recording theme, with time combined
  only among apps assigned to the same theme. Existing records retain theirs.
- Crystals represent total credited time: 250 minutes makes one crystal, ten
  make the next size, and remaining time carries forward. Original records,
  dates, UUIDs, and credited totals are retained. No new CloudKit schema or
  source-data migration is required.
- Home separates moving gems from the total-time meter and provides direct
  access to accumulated time and original records. Settings display selected
  app names and improve large-text and narrow-screen layouts.
- Three synthesized alarm sounds and five haptic patterns are added. The
  sounds are generated by the existing source implementation; no new audio
  asset or third-party recording is bundled. `Docs/LICENSE_AUDIT.md` records
  their provenance.
- Time-based crystals do not offer the previous Pro month stamps. Session
  dates and monthly records remain available. Existing Pro purchases, custom
  timers, and unlimited learning apps are preserved. Both language release
  notes disclose this change, and listing/IAP descriptions and review notes
  remove the former promise. Website source is prepared; this is not evidence
  that its deployment succeeded.
